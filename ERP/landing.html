Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about sense.

What is sense?

sense is a web-based accounting and business management platform for managing sales, purchases, inventory, parties, expenses, cash & bank and business reports.

Can I use sense from a browser?

Yes. sense is designed as a web application, so your team can use it through a modern browser.

Does sense support sales and purchase documents?

Yes. The platform includes sales invoices, quotations, sale orders, delivery challans, purchase bills, purchase orders, returns and payment workflows.

Can more than one user work in a company?

Yes. sense supports company users with roles and permissions.

Is sense suitable for businesses in Bangladesh?

Yes. The platform supports BDT and workflows commonly used for sales, purchase, inventory and day-to-day business accounting in Bangladesh.
